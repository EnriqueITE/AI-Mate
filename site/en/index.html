In the editor

From reading
to replying.

Catch up on a thread

Enable reader summaries to get the main points of a message. Quotes contained in the body provide context from earlier in the conversation.

Start a reply

Choose the tone and length of a reply. AI Mate inserts a draft into the compose window for you to review before sending.

Refine one passage

Select a few lines, choose a style and review the suggestion. Copy it, apply it to the selection or undo the last change.

Privacy

What goes
to OpenAI.

Your key and preferences are stored in your Thunderbird profile. Each request goes directly to the OpenAI API with your key and is subject to your account’s terms.

When rewriting a selection
The selected passage, its link destinations, the style and any instructions for that rewrite are sent.
When drafting or improving a reply
The request includes draft text, quoted text, signature context, subject, To/Cc recipients and writing instructions.
