How AI Mate uses your data

When you generate or improve a draft, AI Mate sends the relevant email content, subject, recipients, your instruction and writing preferences to OpenAI using your API key. Summaries send the relevant message content and summary settings. Undo runs locally and does not contact OpenAI. No email data is transmitted without an API key.
